Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Parent Answers

What is the student-to-teacher ratio at Little Stars?
We maintain very low ratios ranging from 1:4 in our Toddler Playgroup up to 1:10 in Senior KG, ensuring every child receives individualized love, guidance, and attention.
Are meals and snacks provided by the school?
Yes! Our in-house certified chef prepares hot, organic, nut-free snacks and balanced lunches fresh every morning. We accommodate all dietary requirements.
What security and safety measures are in place?
Our campus is fully gated with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric secure check-ins, and trained security personnel at all entry points.
Can parents schedule a visit before enrolling?
Absolutely! You can use our "Book a Visit" tab above or contact us directly to schedule a guided tour of our classrooms and play areas.
Prefer to speak on the phone? Call our principal directly at (555) 234-5678 (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm).
