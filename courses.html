Ages 2 - 3 Years

Playgroup Course Details

Our Playgroup provides a joyful first transition from home to school. We nurture toddler independence, sensory awareness, and peer bonding through interactive rhymes, soft sensory materials, and guided free play.
Teacher Ratio: 1 Teacher : 4 Toddlers
Timing: 8:30 AM - 11:30 AM

Core Learning Focus & Skills

  • Social interaction & sharing
  • Basic communication & words
  • Play-based sensory learning
  • Fine & gross motor skill development

Sample Daily Routine

08:30 AM – Warm Welcome & Free Play Center
09:15 AM – Morning Circle, Rhymes & Hello Song
10:00 AM – Sensory Craft & Fine Motor Games
11:30 AM – Story Time & Happy Goodbye
