Our Story & Heart

Nurturing Wonder in Every Little Heart

Little Stars began with a simple belief: childhood should be filled with wonder, play, and security. We created a school that feels like a natural extension of home—full of soft light, living plants, storybook corners, and gentle guidance.

We know that the ages between 2 and 6 are the most formative in human development. Our curriculum seamlessly blends tactile Montessori materials, Reggio Emilia self-expression, and phonics mastery so that every child builds emotional resilience and social happiness.
